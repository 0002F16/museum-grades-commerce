01 — The Brand

A catalogue, not a clearance.
A standard, not a store.

Museum Grades presents the world's finest pre-owned luxury handbags as a curated catalogue — each piece authenticated, condition-graded, and documented with the rigour of an archive.

Our design language is deliberately quiet. Monochrome, generous in whitespace, and free of ornament, it lets the object speak. The interface recedes so the collection can lead.

GradeEvery piece condition-rated
VerifyAuthenticated before listing

Mission. To give pre-owned luxury the catalogue it deserves — trustworthy, beautiful, and built for the discerning collector.

Personality. Discreet. Exact. Confident without volume. We sound like a curator, never a salesperson.

02 — The Wordmark

The Wordmark

Museum Grades is a typographic identity. The wordmark is set in Metropolis Bold, uppercase, with a wide 3–6px letter-spacing. There is no icon — the name, set with discipline, is the mark.

A monochrome palette

The system is built on ink and paper. Greys carry hierarchy; two restrained accents — a soft blush and a verification green — appear only in functional moments. Colour is never decorative.

Museum Grades 06
06 — Voice & Tone

How we sound

“The world's finest pre-owned luxury handbags — curated, authenticated, and catalogued.”

Curatorial

We describe, we don't sell. Precise nouns over adjectives. Let the object earn the admiration.

Exact

Specific and verifiable. Conditions are graded, not gushed about. Trust is built on accuracy.

Quiet

No exclamation, no hype, no urgency tricks. Confidence speaks at a measured volume.

Words we use
CuratedAuthenticatedGradedAcquisitionCatalogueCollectorProvenanceConsidered
Words we avoid
CheapDealHurrySale!!!Must-haveInsaneLimited time

Say: “Featured Acquisitions.”   “Authenticated before listing.”   “Condition: Excellent.”

Not: “Hot deals!”   “Grab it before it's gone!”   “Unbeatable prices!”
